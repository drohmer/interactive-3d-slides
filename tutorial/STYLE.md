# Writing style of the tutorial

The tone follows the lab pages of Damien Rohmer (EPITA Ani3D, CSC 43043 at Ecole polytechnique).
Only the tone: the layout of the pages (sections, lists, buttons, the way links appear) stays as it is.

- Plain, technical, neutral; no promotional turn of phrase ("live in the slide while you speak",
  "what you will make").
- Complete sentences rather than clipped fragments ("This first example is a cube that rotates by
  itself", not "The smallest program for a slide: a cube that turns").
- State what a page or example does: "This example displays ...", "In this example, ...".
- Precisions in parentheses; "you" for the reader, impersonal forms when natural.
- French version in the same tone, not word for word (vous, "Cet exemple ...", "Rem.").
